About Me

I'm a third-year Computer Science undergraduate at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, focused on applied LLM systems and reliable, cost-efficient AI infrastructure. My work centers on making language models produce correct, executable output - pairing deterministic programmatic logic (graph algorithms, schema constraints, execution validation) with LLMs to cut hallucinations and guarantee results. My text-to-SQL system, Elysian-SQL, ranks #60 on the public BIRD benchmark (66.46% execution accuracy on the private test set) - built on an open-weight model, single-pass, with no fine-tuning, for under $0.50 per full run. I've also shipped a production NL2SQL system end-to-end for a retail enterprise as the sole engineer. I'm especially interested in ML systems, inference efficiency, and architecting AI pipelines that run reliably and cheaply at scale.
